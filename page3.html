TYPES OF FASHION

Discover Different Styles and Fashion Trends

Western Fashion

Western fashion includes modern dresses, jeans, tops, jackets and stylish outfits.

Traditional Fashion

Traditional fashion represents culture through sarees, suits, lehengas and ethnic wear.

Casual Fashion

Casual fashion focuses on comfortable everyday clothing like T-shirts and jeans.

Luxury Fashion

Luxury fashion includes designer outfits, premium fabrics and elegant styles.
