Introduction to Fashion

Fashion is an important part of our daily life. It represents our personality, culture, creativity and lifestyle. Fashion includes clothing, footwear, accessories and different styles.

The fashion industry is a large industry that includes designing, manufacturing, marketing and selling clothes and accessories.

Today, fashion trends change with time. Designers create new styles according to people's interests, seasons and cultures.

Fashion is a way to express yourself!
